How it works

Overview

Sun heats salty water. Vapour cools on the glass and drips into a channel. Filters and UV treat the water before it is stored.

Process sequence

1Sun heats the water.
2Water vapour rises; salt stays behind.
3Vapour cools on the glass and drips.
4Filters and UV treat the water.
5Cleaned water flows into the tank.
